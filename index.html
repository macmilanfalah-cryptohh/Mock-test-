Part 1: Reading Comprehension (1 Single Passage • 5 Questions • 1.0 mark each = 5 Marks)

Reading Passage: Adapting to University Life

Entering university is one of the most significant transitions in a student's academic journey. Unlike high school, where teachers closely monitor daily assignments, attendance, and deadlines, higher education requires a high degree of self-reliance, independence, and effective time management. University students are expected to manage their own learning, attend lectures regularly, complete independent readings, and prepare for major examinations like midterms and finals.

Academic advisors emphasize that establishing a structured weekly schedule early in the semester is crucial for success. Successful university students usually prioritize their academic commitments while allocating sufficient time for extracurricular activities, exercise, and social interaction. Furthermore, taking advantage of campus support resources—such as the library, writing centers, and professor office hours—can substantially improve academic performance and significantly reduce stress levels.

Writing Prompt: High School vs. University Life

Evaluated by automated AI assistant for grammar, coherence, transitions, and vocabulary.

Instructions:

Write a well-structured compare and contrast paragraph (100 – 150 words) comparing high school and university life.

Requirements:

  • Include a clear topic sentence.
  • Discuss both similarities (e.g. studying, taking exams) and differences (e.g. independence, schedule flexibility, teacher support).
  • Use appropriate transition signals (e.g., however, on the other hand, similarly, in contrast, whereas).
  • Provide a concluding sentence summarizing the main takeaway.
